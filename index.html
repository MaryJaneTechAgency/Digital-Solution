Selected work

Three projects I designed, with what I worked on and the thinking behind each one.

01 Fashion — Womenswear

VICI

A women's fashion storefront built around a fast-moving catalogue: new arrivals, occasion edits and frequent promotional events.

Design approach
Give promotions a clear, branded stage without losing the catalogue. The homepage leads with the current offer, while a full category bar lets shoppers who already know what they want go straight to dresses, sets or occasion wear.
My contribution
  • Homepage layout and visual hierarchy
  • Navigation and category structure
  • Promotional and campaign sections
  • Collection grid presentation
Design features
  • Offer-led hero with one clear action
  • Shipping-threshold announcement bar
  • Full category navigation
  • Lifestyle imagery into collections

Live Fit Apparel

An athletic streetwear label with a bold, confident identity and graphic-led products.

03 Luxury fashion — Multi-designer

Cettire

A multi-designer luxury fashion marketplace, where product photography has to feel like an editorial spread.

Design approach
Remove anything that competes with the clothes. A centred wordmark, a short department menu and generous whitespace give a large, image-first grid room to breathe — the restraint itself signals luxury.
My contribution
  • Homepage layout and image grid
  • Header and department navigation
  • Typography and spacing system
  • Editorial presentation of collections
Design features
  • Centred, minimal header
  • Department-led navigation
  • Large editorial image tiles
  • Neutral palette, restrained type

Design decisions with a commercial reason.

    What you getA store built properly from day one: a theme shaped around your brand, collections and navigation planned for your catalogue, carefully chosen apps, and full launch preparation and testing.

    The problemVisitors arrive, browse, and leave without adding to cart or finishing checkout.

How I work

A clear process, from brief to launch.

  1. 01

    Discovery

  2. 02

    Design

    Layouts for the pages that do the selling — homepage, collections and product pages — designed for desktop and mobile, with revisions included.

Questions

Before we start.

How long does a Shopify store take?

A standard store build usually takes 5–14 days, depending on the design complexity and how many products need setting up. Larger redesigns are scheduled after discovery, and you'll have a timeline before any work starts.

Can you redesign my existing store?

Yes. I start by reviewing your current store to find what's holding it back, then rebuild those parts while keeping your products, URLs and search visibility intact.

Are revisions included?

Yes — up to three rounds of design revisions are included in every project.

How is pricing worked out?

Every project is quoted after a short discovery conversation, once I understand your store, your catalogue and what you need. The enquiry form below is the quickest way to start.

Do you work on platforms other than Shopify?

Shopify is my focus. I've also built stores on WordPress/WooCommerce, Wix and Webflow, so ask if your project is on one of those.

Can I hire you through Upwork?

Yes. Many clients prefer Upwork for its payment protection and milestone tracking. You can also contact me directly by email.

About

I design stores the way shoppers actually use them.

I'm Opeyemi Mary Adebayo — Mary to most clients — a Shopify Partner who designs and builds ecommerce stores, mostly for fashion, apparel and lifestyle brands.

My work sits between two questions: does this look like the brand, and does it make buying easy? A beautiful homepage that hides the size guide isn't finished. Neither is a fast checkout on a store that looks like a template.

So I start with your products and your customers, not a theme. I plan navigation around how people really browse your catalogue, design the pages that carry the sale, and then build them in Shopify myself.

I'm based in Nigeria and work remotely with brands in the US, UK and Europe, keeping hours that overlap with yours.
